MISFITS SHELF TALKERS
=====================
Retail shelf talkers for Misfits: Classic Edition. Three designs, four sizes.

THE THREE DESIGNS
  01 The Next Classic Stacking Game   hand and stack on orange
  02 A Designers Love Letter          friends around a blue table
  03 Deceptively Simple               quote from Leslie Scott, inventor of Jenga

FOLDERS
  BC Ferries (with Designed in BC banner)
      Letter 8.5 x 11 in
      5 x 7 in
  Other retailers (no banner)
      A4 210 x 297 mm
      A5 148 x 210 mm
  Previews        small JPEG of every design and size

Inside each size folder:
  the PDFs                       cut to the finished size. Use these for an office printer.
  With bleed (for print shops)   same designs with extra bleed around the edge (5 mm for Letter and 5 x 7, 3 mm for A4 and A5).

PRINTING
  - Print at 100% ("Actual size"), not "Fit to page".
  - Office printers leave a white border around the edge. The photos run to the edge of the card, so a border is expected.
  - Colours are CMYK (US Web Coated SWOP v2). The orange prints a little duller than it looks on screen. Do a test print first.

QR CODES
  Each design has its own QR code, so scans can be told apart. They point to misfits.co.
